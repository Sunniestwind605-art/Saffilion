# Saffilion answer-bank sources

The answer bank is hand-curated. Rarity is an editorial estimate of how familiar an answer is **within its prompt**, not a claim about live player popularity. Sources establish whether answers fit their questions; they do not determine rarity automatically.

Research reviewed for the November 2026 expansion:

- [United Nations Statistics Division M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) for current country and area names and geographic groupings.
- [European Council: countries using the euro](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/topics/the-euro/) and the [European Central Bank’s Bulgaria changeover page](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/bulgaria/html/index.en.html) for the 21-member euro area after Bulgaria joined on 1 January 2026.
- [NASA: About the Planets](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/) for the exact eight-planet set.
- [IUPAC Periodic Table](https://iupac.org/what-we-do/periodic-table-of-elements/) for chemical element names.
- [South African Government: South Africa’s people](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/south-africas-people) for the 12 official languages, including South African Sign Language.
- [South African Government: South Africa at a glance](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/south-africa-glance-1) and [South Africa’s provinces](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/south-africas-provinces) for province, capital, language, and national context.
- [Olympic Studies Centre swimming overview](https://library.olympics.com/digitalCollection/DigitalCollectionAttachmentDownloadHandler.ashx?documentId=158773&parentDocumentId=71295&skipCopyright=true&skipWatermark=true) for the four Olympic competition strokes.

Every build validates question IDs, tier scores, normalized aliases, and duplicates. Tests also lock the exact sizes of factual finite sets so future expansions cannot quietly add invalid answers.
